## The order (current; supersedes every earlier ordering)

Paraphrased: development speed first, token saving second, everything else third — but without starvation; once the
high-impact speed and token items are done, the other work must get attention too.

1. **Dev speed** — the high-impact speed rows of the backlog.
2. **Token saving** — the high-impact token rows.
3. **Others** — publishing the tooling as a plugin, the product itself, comprehensibility follow-ups.

**Anti-starvation is part of the rule:** once the HIGH-IMPACT items of 1 and 2 are done, 3 gets real attention — do
not keep mining low-impact speed/token rows to stay in lanes 1–2.

**Still standing from an earlier ordering (not superseded):** work that needs the owner's time (walkthroughs, anything
that lets them steer) is done WHILE THEY ARE PRESENT; autonomous lanes run while they are AFK. Paraphrased: if the
owner cannot follow what is being done or where the work stands, local speed means little — it can be
tactically fast while heading the wrong way, strategically or operationally. Phase 1 is the human gate
([[document-approval-is-interactive]]).

**How to apply:** pick from lane 1, then 2; check whether their high-impact items are done before preferring a
low-impact one over lane 3. Budget mode: [[behavior-follows-the-budget-situation]].

## How the order came about (paraphrased)

- An earlier ordering put the comprehensibility work and publishing it as a plugin first, speed next and the
  original product third; it replaced an even earlier "token usage first" ordering.
- Speed was then raised above the rest, and the work was framed as one goal with two lanes: DIRECTION
  (comprehensibility, done while the owner is present) and THROUGHPUT (speed rows, done while they are away).
- A walkthrough settled several open items, including how to split "needs you" into *blocking now* and *waiting until
  picked*, and that backlog readability should improve through layout (line breaks, indenting) without rewording.
- The current order was first recorded wrongly (plugin before token) and the owner corrected it to the order above.
