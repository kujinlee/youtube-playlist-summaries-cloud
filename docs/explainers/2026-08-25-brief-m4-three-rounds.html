The decision

Stop planning M4 in its current shape. Round 4 would be the fourth non-converging round — which is exactly when dev-process.md says to escalate from fix to redesign.

Three rounds, and rounds 2 and 3 each refuted the previous round's fix. That is the documented Phase 6 trigger, bought with twelve rounds on this very spec. The plan does not need a fourth patch; the milestone needs re-scoping — because B2 and B4 say its central staging idea cannot execute at all, and B1 says its task list was written from a file count rather than the files.

The strongest argument against

Escalation is expensive and the findings are all fixable: promote three files, add one revoke, drop M4-α. That is an afternoon, not a redesign — and Phase 6 on a milestone that has not written a line of SQL risks re-litigating ADR-0006 and ADR-0007, which were accepted yesterday and which dev-process.md forbids re-opening.

The answer: that argument is right about the ADRs and wrong about the plan. Phase 6 here is not a design review of the accepted schema — it is asking why three rounds of planning produced a task list whose first instruction was to ship delete from profiles. The likely finding is small and structural: M4 was scoped as “promote four files” when the real unit is “make an interdependent schema execute against a database that has the right shape and data”. Those are different milestones.

Fold round 3 into a v4 and keep going — or re-scope M4 first?
