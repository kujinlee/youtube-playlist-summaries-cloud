The decision

Nine days, 20 commits, zero product lines, and no verifiable deployment in 39 days. The product is a YouTube playlist summariser. None of this work touched it.

The case for continuing — and it is measured, not felt

In one session, four separate guards caught four defects their own author had missed: an under-varied test parameter, a NO-REVIEW: token written as a heading so the parser could not see it, two roadmap items sharing a name so one tick contradicted the other, and an unescaped | that silently shifted a table column. None was caught by review or by care.

Above that, a Phase 6 architecture review found a defect class that nine rounds of adversarial review could not — that the standard repair was generating the next instance of the bug it fixed — and the fix had already existed in this repository for weeks, in one file, reaching zero others. That is a compounding return: the machinery is now finding faults in itself faster than review finds them.

The case against — also measured

158 rows open against 60 closed. The backlog is growing, and it is growing from the tooling: of today’s three new rows, all three are about the verification system, and one of them (#218) exists only because an explainer page could not obey its own delivery contract. Tooling that generates tooling work has no natural stopping point, and nothing on this page shows the product moving.

The sharpest form of the objection: you cannot currently prove the product is running at all. Thirty-nine days of unverified deployment is a bigger risk than any open 🟠 row.

Recommendation

Authenticate flyctl and verify production before any further tooling work. It is minutes, it answers the one question this briefing could not, and until it is answered every other priority is a guess. The 39-day gap is not evidence of a problem — it is the absence of evidence either way, which is exactly the state this project’s own rules say must never be read as a pass.

After that: finish D1 and D2 — they are small, specified, and they close the class — then stop and give the product a slice. The verification investment has earned its keep on the measurements above, but it should now be asked to protect something that is shipping.
