The short version

The server shows your folder, not master. Your folder stopped following master once you started editing a page there, because git refuses to overwrite your edits. Everything merged after that point is invisible on 7391. The fix: the server reads a separate, untouched copy of master that updates itself, and your folder is only shown on top of it when that's safe.

The one loose end

When you answer a question on a page with Ask, the answer is written into the page file in your folder. That counts as a local edit, so the page would show the yellow bar until the answer is committed. The fix belongs to the Ask-answerer work (backlog #625, planned for Friday): answers go on a small branch and merge, and then they're on master like everything else.
