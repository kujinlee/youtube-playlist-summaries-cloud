The decision

Write the M4 plan next — and flip ADR-0010 to accepted on the way past.

M4 is bounded: four SQL files become migrations 0027+, landing inert with no application caller. It turns two red ratchets green by giving them a real schema to read, and it is the step that makes M5 possible.

The strongest argument against

M4 puts video_artifacts and video_generations into production Postgres, and production holds paid Gemini content. This schema has never executed anywhere but inside a review's rollback — its assertions have never run against a real database and its mutation harness has never scored against one. Landing it “inert” is safe only if inert is true, and that is a claim about every caller, not about the migration.

The answer: that is precisely why it goes first and alone. Inert is checkable — generation_id appears in 2 files outside docs/ today, so “no caller reaches it” is a grep, not a hope. And the alternative is worse: another round of design review would again verify a schema that has never run, which is the exact critique that ended round 17.
