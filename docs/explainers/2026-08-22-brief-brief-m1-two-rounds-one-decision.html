The decision

Both rounds trace to one cause: M1 was scoped as "the two omitted fields" when the real problem is that the whole twelve-field card is written unconditionally for a body that may never have been published. Gating two of twelve is a symptom patch, and the coherent version touches money — withholding docVersion stops the idempotency skip firing, so the next attempt re-runs Gemini and charges again.

✓ CHOSEN 2026-08-22 — skip M1, start at backlog #23. M1 is re-scoped, not deleted. The decision. Real work, no entanglement, and it is the slice's only genuine blocker. The live harm stays until M5 — but it has been live for months already, and M5 dissolves it properly rather than patching around it.
Option 1 — re-scope M1 to "the card is written as a unit, gated" Coherent, and it would close backlog #22's row-lies-about-docVersion half as a side effect. But it is a money-path slice needing its own spec — not the warm-up M1 was supposed to be.
Option 3 — narrow M1 to mdCorrectionsHash alone That one field genuinely has no fallback, so gating it works. Fixes half of #23(a) for a few lines. But it leaves a half-gated card — a third state to reason about, which is the "individually thoughtful, fails as a set" pattern this repo keeps measuring.

The strongest argument against the recommendation

Option 2 knowingly leaves user-typed corrections being silently destroyed in production. "It has been broken for months" is the reasoning that lets defects age indefinitely, and #23 is an M + design item — a spec, a redesign of how corrections are represented, and re-authoring 99 existing records. That is weeks before the live harm stops, when Option 3 could stop half of it this evening.

The answer

The harm's blast radius is bounded and known: it needs a cloud re-summarize of a video that has local corrections. Option 3 buys a partial fix on that narrow path at the cost of a third card state that every future reader of persist_summary must hold in their head — and two rounds just demonstrated how expensive a half-understood card state is. The cheap fix is only cheap if you stop there, and the evidence of this afternoon is that we do not.
